Customer Interview Report: Medical App

Date: August 25, 2026
Local: Pennsylvania, USA
Participant: Alex, 34, patient who manages regular medical appointments

Purpose
The interview explored how patients manage health information, appointments, and medication reminders.

Key Findings
1. Alex currently uses a calendar, paper notes, and several clinic websites to manage healthcare tasks.
2. The participant wants one secure medical app for appointments, prescriptions, test results, notifications, and doctor messages.
3. Medication reminders are useful, but the participant wants reminders that can be postponed without losing them.
4. Alex is concerned about privacy and expects clear explanations of how personal health data is stored and shared.
5. Medical language can be confusing. The participant would value simple explanations and the ability to contact a healthcare professional.

User Needs
The medical app should provide a simple dashboard, appointment notifications, medication reminders, access to test results, and secure communication with healthcare providers. Important actions should be easy to find and accessible on a mobile phone.

Conclusion
The interview suggests that patients need a trustworthy and easy-to-use medical app that brings common healthcare tasks together. Privacy, accessibility, clear information, and reliable reminders should be priorities for the first version.
